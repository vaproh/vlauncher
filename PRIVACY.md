# vlauncher privacy

vlauncher is a fork of [Olauncher](https://github.com/tanujnotes/Olauncher) and keeps its offline-first behavior.

- No analytics, telemetry, ads, or accounts.
- No personal data is collected or shared by vlauncher.
- The internet permission is used only to download the optional daily wallpaper, plus opening links you tap in settings.
- App usage data, when you grant usage access for screen time, stays on the device.

The upstream project's own privacy policy is at https://github.com/tanujnotes/Olauncher. This fork makes no additional network requests.
